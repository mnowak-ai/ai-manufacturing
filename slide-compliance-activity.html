Does the AI Have the Full Picture?
AI sees patterns in data. It doesn't automatically see everything that matters. Tap to check what might be missing.
🔧
Check recent repair history
This machine had a minor repair three weeks ago. The AI's performance data doesn't flag anything unusual right now — but a recent repair is exactly the kind of detail an inspection is meant to confirm went well.
📜
Check company policy on delays
Company policy allows inspection delays only with supervisor sign-off and only when no recent repairs or incidents have occurred. Neither condition has been confirmed yet.
⚠️
Check who's accountable if something goes wrong
If the inspection is skipped and a problem happens afterward, the shift supervisor who approved the delay is accountable — not the AI system. That responsibility doesn't transfer just because a system made the suggestion.
