What This Activity Showed

✓

Good reports answer four questions: what happened, how serious, what's the evidence, and what should happen next — in plain language another team can act on immediately.

✓

AI flags are only part of the story. Human verification, location patterns, and context are what make a report complete and trustworthy.

✓

Unverified rumors, opinions, and staffing notes don't belong in a QC report — they add noise without helping anyone solve the problem faster.
