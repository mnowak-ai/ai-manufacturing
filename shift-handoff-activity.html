AI First Draft — Review for Gaps
"Packaging line experienced delays due to sensor issue. Maintenance resolved problem."
Tap everything that's missing from the draft above:
Missing detail
The fix was temporary — maintenance didn't fully resolve the problem, just made an adjustment to keep production going.
Missing action item
The next shift was told to monitor the sensor closely — that instruction isn't anywhere in the draft.
Missing status
There's no mention of whether the system was operational at end of shift — the next team doesn't know if they're walking into a running line or a stopped one.
Missing specifics
"Delays" is vague — the actual cause was false readings from the sensor causing short pauses. That's specific information a technician would want.
Not a gap
The name of the technician who did the repair — work updates don't typically need to name individuals unless there's a specific accountability reason.
Not a gap
The exact time the sensor started acting up — precise timestamps are useful in logs, but a handoff message focuses on what happened and what to do next.
